How the work gets done

Getting through the nuts and bolts of a problem takes time, experience, energy, and an obsession to get it right.

Clear communication, careful preparation and unwavering advocacy. You work with Mr. Starke himself, and you get honest guidance about where you stand.

What to expect when you call

  1. A first conversation

    You call or write, and you speak with Mr. Starke himself. Tell him what happened in your own words. The conversation is confidential.

  2. A candid assessment

    He tells you plainly where you stand, what your options are, and what each is likely to involve, including cost, before you commit to anything.

  3. A plan for your matter

    Together you choose the course: negotiation, mediation, a board hearing or litigation, with clear deadlines and next steps.

  4. Counsel to the end

    He prepares you for every meeting, mediation and hearing, keeps you informed, and remains available long after the matter closes.
